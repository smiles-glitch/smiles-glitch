# Data Annotation & Quality Assurance — Sample Work

**Focus:** Consistent labels, clear guidelines, and quality checks.

## Objective
Illustrate a simple process for improving annotation consistency across a dataset.

## Workflow
1. Read the task definition and labeling guidelines.
2. Identify the available labels and edge cases.
3. Apply labels consistently using the stated rules.
4. Flag ambiguous or insufficient examples rather than guessing.
5. Review a sample of completed annotations for consistency.
6. Record recurring errors and clarify the guideline where needed.

## Quality checklist
- Does each label match the definition?
- Is the decision supported by the provided data?
- Are edge cases handled consistently?
- Are missing or ambiguous details flagged?
- Can another reviewer understand the rationale?
